## The Four Houses 🏠

Ever had trouble finding your people?
At least a quarter of the world’s population are so similar to you that you and them share the same struggles and triumphs on a daily basis.

| House            | Philosophy                                                                 | Types                  | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Crusaders**    | "Life is about sacrifice and enduring hardship to foster justice/fairness" | ESFJ, ENTP, ISFJ, INTP | Crusaders are dutiful champions of fairness and protecting the innocent. They place their faith in truth and seek to bring about a just world. Crusaders seek to make others happy yet have difficulty accepting happiness for themselves. Through adversity they obtain happiness. Crusaders see hardship as something to persevere through and wield high endurance but are at risk of bitterness or being too cold when dealing out justice. Crusaders are at risk of hypocrisy by finding the innocent guilty and can become a source of injustice.                             |
| **Templars**     | "Life is about strengthening others to mutually grow stronger"             | ESTP, ENFJ, ISTP, INFJ | Templars seek people of character or build character in those who lack it. They are interested in strengthening the well-being and character of others. Templars require freedom to make their own choices and find their own way in life. They teach, mentor, and counsel. They forgive and help people heal, ghosting traitors or people who refuse to take responsibility for themselves. Templars are at risk of hypocrisy when criticizing others for being irresponsible, while being irresponsible themselves.                                                               |
| **Wayfarers**    | "Life is about securing my position to choose worthy companions"           | ENTJ, ESFP, INTJ, ISFP | Wayfarers are independent, realistic, and matter of fact. They challenge the status quo in favor of finding a better way. Wayfarers require freedom to pursue their own interests and way of life. They are self-assured and express themselves through personal performance, often competitively. They seek admiration and loyalty, but doubts others’ ability to provide it. Their purpose is to pursue treasure and the status it brings, choosing with whom they share it. Wayfarers are at risk of stealing treasure, rather than earning it for themselves.                   |
| **Philosophers** | "Life is about discovering the fundamental principles of success"          | ESTJ, ENFP, ISTJ, INFP | Philosophers are academically inclined to discover secrets to success. They craft a world view to share belief, ideology, and reputation. Philosophers are drawn to civic duty, volunteering, and politics to strengthen their voice. Through enduring hardships, Philosophers establish rules and guidelines they believe are righteous. They attend to their own happiness and comfort yet can be inattentive to that of others. Philosophers are at risk of sacrificing fellow human beings for the sake of their own success, instead of success gained through self-sacrifice. |
