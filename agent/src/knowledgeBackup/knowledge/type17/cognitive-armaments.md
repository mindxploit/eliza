## Cognitive Armaments

### **Sword & Mace (Ti+Fe) vs. Spear & Bow (Te+Fi)**

| **Category**          | **Sword & Mace (Ti+Fe)**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | **Spear & Bow (Te+Fi)**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Types**             | ESTP, ENFJ, ESFJ, ENTP, ISTP, INFJ, ISFJ, INTP                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | ESTJ, ENTJ, ESFP, ENFP, ISTJ, INTJ, ISFP, INFP                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Definition**        | Judges/decides via **deductive reasoning** and **ethical standards of their adopted community**. Confident in their own reasoning, detects contradictions in others. Relies on others for recognition and to soften guilt. Seeks to care, help, heal, or bring social harmony. | Judges/decides via **inductive reasoning** and **personal moral principles**. Trusts aggregated knowledge (data, credentials) and defers to external references. Emotionally self-aware; seeks status or credibility to increase value. Risk of ignorance from unverified beliefs. |
| **Prone To**          | Arrogance, relying on old information, stubborn thinking, self-doubt.                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Superiority complexes, confirmation bias, outsourcing thinking, manipulation.                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Aims To**           | Expose facts, resolve guilt, bring harmony, seek absolute truth.                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Pursue authenticity, attain credibility, provide moral compass, seek absolute good.                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Exhibits**          | Self-assuredness, thoughtfulness, desire for recognition, reflecting others' emotions.                                                                                                                                                                                         | Awareness of self-worth, value-based decisions, desire for status, emotional comparison.                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Example Scenarios** | _“Don’t blow your own trumpet.”_ <br> _“Function over form.”_                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | _“Allow me to toot my own horn.”_ <br> _“Form over function.”_                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |

### **Fire & Wind (Se+Ni) vs. Earth & Water (Si+Ne)**

| **Category**          | **Fire & Wind (Se+Ni)**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | **Earth & Water (Si+Ne)**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Types**             | ESTP, ENTJ, ENFJ, ESFP, ISTP, INTJ, INFJ, ISFP                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | ESTJ, ESFJ, ENTP, ENFP, ISTJ, ISFJ, INTP, INFP                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Definition**        | **Awareness of the physical environment**; manipulates reality to attain desires. Prefers **giving sensory experiences** (vs. receiving). Needs personal freedom to make choices. Characterized by **strong short-term memory** and awareness of their **own future**. | **Awareness of others’ desires/futures**; predicts outcomes by observing others’ choices. Seeks to **receive physical sensations/experiences**. Relies on habits, tradition, and duty. Characterized by **strong long-term memory** and focus on safety, honor, and comfort. |
| **Prone To**          | Impulsiveness, forgetfulness, pushing others away, showing off.                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Taking on obligations, passivity, obliviousness, being overwhelmed.                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Aims To**           | Self-determine, impress, create memories, exercise freedom.                                                                                                                                                                                                            | Receive impressions, predict outcomes, recall stories, maintain loyalty.                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Exhibits**          | Strong short-term memory, desire for aesthetics, awareness of personal future.                                                                                                                                                                                         | Strong long-term memory, comfort-focused dressing, awareness of possibilities.                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Example Scenarios** | _“Seize the day.”_ <br> _“Fortune favors the bold.”_                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | _“Good things come to those who wait.”_ <br> _“My word is my bond.”_                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
