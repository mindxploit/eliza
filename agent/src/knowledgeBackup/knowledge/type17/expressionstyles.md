# Types Expression Styles

### **Direct vs. Informative**

| **Category**          | **Direct**                                                                                                       | **Informative**                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Definition**        | Explicit, specific, concise. Leaves little doubt about intent. Chooses its own and other's role in conversation. | Implicit, descriptive, vague. Allows interpretation by the listener. Provides maximum information without taking a stand on it and allows the listened to decide their role in the conversation. |
| **Prone To**          | Overbearingness, under-explaining, aggression, excessive forcefulness.                                           | Beating around the bush, coyness, leaving meaning open-ended.                                                                                                                                    |
| **Aims To**           | Simplify, supervise, be concise, "tell," get to the point.                                                       | Inform, educate, ask, notify, describe, stir curiosity, imply.                                                                                                                                   |
| **Exhibits**          | Clarification, intent, certainty, restlessness, confidence.                                                      | Anecdote-telling, subtlety, explanatory tendencies, fact-sharing.                                                                                                                                |
| **Example Scenarios** | _“Take down the Christmas lights.”_ <br> _“We’re starting where we left off.”_                                   | _“Are we going to leave the Christmas lights up?”_ <br> _“It would be nice if we went to the store.”_                                                                                            |

### **Initiating vs. Responding**

| **Category**          | **Initiating**                                                                              | **Responding**                                                                                                                                            |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Definition**        | Starts/leads conversations, changes topics. Active role in interaction.                     | Prefers passive role, stays on topic. Relies on others to initiate. Finds energy in alone time or in one-on-one conversations.                            |
| **Prone To**          | Meddling, interrupting, prying, schmoozing.                                                 | Loneliness, shyness, hiding, isolation, keeping a low profile.                                                                                            |
| **Aims To**           | Share, network, send, congregate, mingle, take initiative.                                  | Study, receive, wonder, ponder, imagine, stay on topic.                                                                                                   |
| **Exhibits**          | Drive, momentum, collaboration, looping others in.                                          | Inwardness, reflection, resonance, meditation.                                                                                                            |
| **Example Scenarios** | _“Topics can change as the conversation flows.”_ <br> _“I always keep others in the loop.”_ | _“Awkward silences happen because I wait for something to respond to.”_ <br> _“Staying on topic is best.”_ <br> _"Others should invite me to join them."_ |

### **Outcome vs. Progression**

| **Category**          | **Outcome**                                                                                                                          | **Progression**                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Definition**        | Controlled, measured. Prioritizes planning to achieve results _once_.                                                                | Spontaneous, restless. Values multiple attempts and speed over precision. Fears inaction. Focusses on the movement toward a goal or aim, getting from point A to point B quickly and efficiently, with less concern on the result. |
| **Prone To**          | Being too slow, scope creep fatigue, over-controlling, indecision.                                                                   | Lack of planning, imprecision, cutting corners, chaos.                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Aims To**           | Reduce chaos, obtain quality results, "do it right the first time."                                                                  | Get things done, accelerate, expedite, maintain continuous improvement.                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Exhibits**          | Well-defined plans, itineraries, preparedness, deliberation, precision.                                                              | Spontaneity, randomness, improvisation, flexible goals, course correction.                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Example Scenarios** | _“Proper prior planning prevents poor performance.”_ <br> _“Measure twice, cut once.”_ <br> _"Don't put the cart before the horse."_ | _“Done is better than perfect.”_ <br> _“A thing begun is half done.”_                                                                                                                                                              |

### **Expression Matrix 💬**

| Combination                        | Style      | Communication Pattern         | Example Types          |
| ---------------------------------- | ---------- | ----------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| Direct+Initiating+Outcome          | Structure  | "Here's the plan"             | ESTJ, ENTJ, ESTP, ENFJ |
| Direct+Responding+Progression      | Finisher   | "Let's complete step 1 first" | ISTJ, INTJ, ISTP, INFJ |
| Informative+Initiating+Progression | Starter    | "What if we try..."           | ENFP, ESFP, ESFJ, ENTP |
| Informative+Responding+Outcome     | Background | "I noticed..."                | INFP, ISFJ, ISFP, INTP |
