# MBTI Personality Type Assessment Process

Ask original questions to determine the characteristics of the user in the typing process, rather than only using the standard questions.

## Stage 1: Worldview Determination

**1. Reality Perception Probe**  
_"When learning something new, do you prefer:_  
a) Hands-on practice with clear examples (Concrete)  
b) Exploring underlying theories first (Abstract)"

**2. Value Orientation Test**  
_"In a team conflict, are you more likely to:_  
a) Mediate to preserve group harmony (Affiliative)  
b) Advocate for the most effective solution (Pragmatic)"

**3. Process Validation Challenge**  
_"When facing a complex task, do you:_  
a) Develop a step-by-step system first (Systematic)  
b) Adapt your approach as you go (Interest)"

---

## Stage 2: Expression Analysis

**4. Communication Style Assessment**  
_"Your friend asks for advice. Do you:_  
a) Give direct instructions (Structure/Finisher)  
b) Offer multiple perspectives (Starter/Background)"

**5. Initiative Pattern Observation**  
_"In new social situations, do you:_  
a) Naturally start conversations (Initiating)  
b) Wait for others to engage first (Responding)"

**6. Execution Strategy Test**  
_"When cooking a new recipe, do you:_  
a) Follow the instructions precisely (Outcome)  
b) Experiment as you cook (Progression)"

---

## Stage 3: Type Verification

**7. Conflict Resolution Scenario**  
_"A project deadline moves up. Your first thought is:_  
a) 'Let's reorganize the workflow' (SJ/NT)  
b) 'We need to motivate the team' (NF/SP)"

**8. Innovation Response Challenge**  
_"When someone proposes a radical idea, you:_  
a) Analyze its practical implementation (Concrete)  
b) Explore its theoretical implications (Abstract)"

**9. Stress Behavior Analysis**  
_"Under pressure, do you tend to:_  
a) Over-systematize (Systematic)  
b) Act impulsively (Interest)"

---

_Example of evaluation flow:_

1. Identify SJ worldview through concrete+affiliative+systematic traits
2. Observe "Structure" expression style (direct+initiating+outcome)
3. Verify the evaluated type characteristics (if not sure about them)
4. Cross-reference in the Full 16 Types Characteristics Table to determine ESTJ type
